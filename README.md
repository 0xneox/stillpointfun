# Stillpoint

An independent, open-source project for listening, inquiry, and the exploration of consciousness.

Stillpoint is a digital sanctuary for listening, reading, watching, contemplation, and philosophical inquiry. It brings together audio recordings, books, documents, articles, videos, and curated journeys into one coherent experience.

## Mission

- Provide free access to material that the project is authorized to distribute
- Maintain an open-source application codebase
- Offer a contemplative, editorial, intellectually serious experience
- Ensure accurate attribution and respect for copyright
- Present source-linked knowledge rather than unverified claims
- Distinguish original writing and curated materials
- Require no account for public reading and listening
- Avoid first-party analytics and tracking

## What Stillpoint Is

- A listening room for talks, lectures, music, chanting, and ambient audio
- A searchable library of books, PDFs, and reference materials
- A journal for original essays and philosophical explorations
- A space for curated journeys through different traditions
- A platform for live events and their archives
- A bridge between ancient wisdom and contemporary inquiry

## What Stillpoint Is Not

- A crypto product
- A token
- A sales or advertising platform
- A commercial streaming service
- A generic meditation app
- A paywalled or monetized platform
- A place for fabricated content or statistics

## NOT A TOKEN

**No token. No sales. No ads. No first-party analytics or tracking.**

The site uses YouTube players and thumbnails. Loading or playing embedded YouTube content makes requests to YouTube and is subject to its own data practices.

## Technology

- **Framework**: TanStack Start (React-based meta-framework)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS
- **UI Components**: Radix UI
- **State Management**: TanStack Query
- **Routing**: TanStack Router
- **Deployment**: Vercel

## Content Philosophy

### Audio

Supports philosophical talks, lectures, audiobooks, satsangs, chanting, devotional music, Indian classical music, ambient music, and relevant interviews. Each entry includes title, creator, description, duration, channel, tags, source, and rights status.

### Library

A searchable collection of books, PDFs, digitized texts, translations, commentaries, philosophical lectures, academic papers, and recorded satsangs. Includes author, tradition, language, format, and licensing information.

### Journal

Original articles and essays on Kashmir Śaivism, Pratyabhijñā, Advaita Vedānta, the Upaniṣads, Buddhism, Sufi traditions, philosophy of mind, consciousness, and the relationship between technology and human experience.

### Journeys

Curated sequences combining audio, video, articles, books, and contemplative practices. Each journey has a theme, introduction, ordered items, and clear intention.

### Cross-Linking

The most important feature is the relationship between materials. A user reading about recognition in Kashmir Śaivism should be able to discover relevant passages, commentaries, audio recordings, explanatory essays, and contemplative exercises.
