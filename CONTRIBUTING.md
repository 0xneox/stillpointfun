# Contributing to Stillpoint

Corrections, citations, translation references, and open questions are welcome. Please distinguish a correction from an interpretation, include a source and location where possible, and mark uncertain rights or attribution for verification.

The Path is written in the author's voice. Contributions may identify errors, offer references, and raise questions, but Path steps are not co-authored. Do not rewrite or extend a Path step in a pull request.

## Content, provenance, and rights

Content uses Markdown with YAML front matter under `content/`. Draft material stays out of the public build. Do not set an item to `published` until its metadata, sources, rights, safety notes, and required review are complete. See [CONTENT.md](./CONTENT.md) for the registry and rights model.

Keep changes focused. Include citations and source locations when proposing factual or translation corrections, and leave open questions explicitly open rather than guessing.
